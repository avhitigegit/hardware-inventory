The Problem Today

Real challenges that cost your business time and money every single day

02 / 10
Most hardware and trade shops across Sri Lanka are still managing their business with paper books, handwritten receipts, and guesswork. Every day this continues, the business is losing money, missing sales opportunities, and falling behind competitors who have moved to digital. Here is what really happens on the shop floor — and the real cost of each problem:
📒

Stock Tracked in Paper Books

Items are written by hand in ledgers or recorded on scraps of paper. When a customer asks "do you have this item?" — the staff has to physically walk to the store and check. This wastes time, frustrates customers, and causes queues to build up at the counter.

⏱ Wastes 30–60 minutes every day
❓

No Warning Before Stock Runs Out

The only time you discover that an item is finished is when a customer asks for it — and by then it is already a lost sale. There is no system giving an early warning. Items run out silently, and customers go to a competitor instead. This happens multiple times a day without anyone realizing it.

💸 Revenue lost silently every week
🧾

Handwritten Bills and Receipts

Every invoice is written by hand — slow to produce, easy to make mistakes on, and looks unprofessional to customers. There is no permanent digital record of what was sold, to whom, and on which date. Billing disputes are impossible to resolve. Tax audits become a nightmare.

😤 Disputes & audit problems
📊

No Reports — Running Blind

Business owners cannot answer basic questions: "What was my best selling product last month?" or "How much profit did I make this week?" There is no data. All purchasing decisions are based on memory and gut feeling — leading to over-stocking slow items and running short on fast-moving ones.

📉 Wrong buying decisions cost money
🔑

No Staff Access Control

Every staff member can see everything — supplier prices, profit margins, and business financials. Cashiers can change prices without authorization. There is no record of who made a sale, who deleted a product, or who changed a price. This creates opportunities for manipulation and theft that go undetected.

🚨 Theft & unauthorized changes
📦

Purchases Not Properly Recorded

When stock arrives from a supplier, it is noted on a paper or ignored entirely. There is no formal GRN document, no stock update trail, and no way to verify if the correct quantity was received. Supplier disputes over short deliveries cannot be resolved because there is no proof of what was agreed.

📦 Supplier disputes go unresolved

User Roles & Access Control

Every staff member sees only what they need — nothing more, nothing less

06 / 10
In most shops, every staff member has access to everything — prices, profits, supplier costs, and cash data. This is a serious risk. The NUVI system uses role-based access — you assign each staff member a specific role when you create their account, and the system automatically restricts what they can see and do. A cashier cannot view your profit margins. A store keeper cannot access cash reports. You remain in complete control of who sees what — without having to lock computers or stand over people's shoulders.
ADMIN

System Administrator

The technical owner of the entire system. Has unrestricted access to every feature, every report, and every setting across the platform. Typically the business owner or a trusted senior manager.

  • Create, edit, and deactivate all user accounts with role assignment
  • Full access to all modules — products, sales, purchases, returns, adjustments
  • View and download all report types including financial and cash summaries
  • Access and review the complete audit log of all system activity
  • Configure product categories, supplier lists, and system settings
  • View cost prices, selling prices, and profit margins on all products
⚠️ This role should only be assigned to the business owner or a fully trusted person — it has no restrictions.
OWNER

Business Owner

Designed for the shop owner who wants full visibility into the business — revenue, stock levels, purchasing history, and performance data — without needing to manage user accounts or system settings.

  • Live dashboard showing today's sales totals, purchase activity, and low stock count
  • Access and download all 5 report types as PDF — daily, monthly, top products, low stock, cash
  • View the full history of all sales, purchases, returns, and stock adjustments
  • Manage products, categories, customers, and supplier directory
  • Monitor staff activity and accountability through the audit log
  • View cost prices and profit margins — confidential financial data fully visible
💡 Ideal for a working owner who is present in the shop and wants to stay informed about all business activity.
CASHIER

Cashier / Sales Staff

Built for counter-level billing staff who handle sales and customer interaction. Has just enough access to do the job efficiently — and nothing more. Sensitive financial information is completely hidden from this role.

  • Full access to the dedicated POS billing terminal for fast checkout
  • Search and view products — names, selling prices, and stock availability only (no cost prices)
  • Process customer returns with a return receipt at the counter
  • Create and view customer records and contact details
  • Cannot access any financial reports, cash summaries, or monthly data
  • Cannot change any product prices, stock quantities, or supplier information
🔒 Cashiers cannot see profit margins, cost prices, or owner-level reports — full counter access with financial privacy protected.
STORE KEEPER

Store Keeper

For the staff member responsible for the physical warehouse or stock room. Their role is entirely focused on receiving deliveries, maintaining accurate stock records, and flagging discrepancies — nothing related to sales or cash.

  • View all products and current stock levels across categories
  • Record incoming stock deliveries and automatically generate GRN documents
  • Perform and document stock adjustments for damages, write-offs, and corrections
  • Access the low stock report to identify what needs to be reordered urgently
  • Cannot access sales figures, cash reports, or customer billing history
  • Cannot change product selling prices or access any financial information
📦 The store keeper role keeps warehouse staff productive and accountable — without any exposure to sensitive sales or financial data.
